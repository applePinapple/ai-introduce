See it. Dodge it

Floors in busy homes are a minefield of socks, toys, mugs, and more. Structured light 3D scanning accurately identifies the location and size of obstacles and hazards, in both bright and dark rooms, for worry-free cleaning at any time of day.

* Based on internal testing carried out by the manufacturer, S7 MaxV can see and bypass objects down to as small as 5cm (2in) wide, and 3cm (1.1in) tall. Recognition accuracy may vary depending on environmental factors.

Recognize and React

Many things can trap a robot, from scales to wires, shoes, and even pet waste. Powered by machine learning and a dedicated neural processing unit, S7 MaxV recognizes these hazards and reacts differently based on what it sees. Getting up close to dropped shoes but staying far away from pet waste. Even threading its way carefully between hazards.
